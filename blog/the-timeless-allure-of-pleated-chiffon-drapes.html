The Timeless Allure of Pleated Chiffon Drapes

By Fashion Atelier Designer • 7 Min Read

From ancient Grecian stoles to modern red carpet gowns, **Pleated Silk Chiffon** creates dramatic kinetic movement. Micro-pleated sunray skirts capture movement gracefully with every step.

1. Sunray Pleating Precision

Paper pattern molds and steam pressing heat-set sharp pleats that expand gracefully into a full skirt circumference when walking.

2. Weightless Fluidity

Silk chiffon's sheer, light weave provides voluminous silhouette structure without adding heavy bulk to eveningwear dresses.
