Understanding Mulberry Silk Momme Weight for Eveningwear

By Silk Textile Specialist • 8 Min Read

**Momme (mm)** is the traditional unit of weight measurement for real silk fabrics. It measures the density of silk threads per 100 yards of fabric.

1. 19 Momme vs. 22 Momme Silk

While 19 Momme silk is ideal for lightweight scarves, luxury haute couture evening gowns demand **22 Momme Grade 6A Mulberry Silk** for rich opacity, substantial drape, and tear resistance.

2. Luster & Breathability

Heavier 22 Momme silk weaves capture light with a soft liquid sheen while remaining hypoallergenic and breathable against skin.
