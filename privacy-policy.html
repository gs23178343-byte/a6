Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026 • Graceful Style Archive Concierge

At **Graceful Style Archive**, accessible from gracefulstylearchive.com, protecting the privacy of our sartorial and haute couture clients is paramount. This document details how we handle user data.

1. Information Collection

When you request atelier consultations or acquire couture pieces, we may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, and delivery preferences.

2. Use of Information

Collected details are used exclusively to process archive orders, manage white-glove delivery, fulfill tailoring guarantees, and provide garment care instructions.
